To use this effect, copy the files to your Aircrafts Models/Effects folder.
Edit reflect-uber.eff to point the paths of the .png files to your aircrafts folder

Add the following lines to your Aircraft set.xml:-

<rendering>
    
<refl_correction type="string">-0.3</refl_correction>
    <!-- edit this to alter the strength of the reflection -->
</rendering>

This needs to go before the </sim> tags in your set file. 

Add the following to your aircraft model.xml:-

<effect>
    
<inherits-from>Aircraft/AS350/Models/Effects/reflect-uber</inherits-from>

<object-name>FUSELAGE PART HERE</object-name> <!-- edit the <object-name> fields to contain all the fuselage parts that are to be included in the reflection -->
<object-name>FUSELAGE PART HERE</object-name>
	
<object-name>FUSELAGE PART HERE</object-name>
	 
<object-name>FUSELAGE PART HERE</object-name>
  
</effect>
